## What the pilot exposed

The first review briefly confused preimplementation scope with an implemented defect. A manually repeated digest and one scenario's wording needed correction. The first product handoff incorrectly inferred that viewport controls were unavailable; documented capability discovery enabled the missing matrix. A focus intent needed one-shot consumption. A reuse/enrollment label still implied creating another profile. The lead also identified mistakes in its own intermediate timing records and excluded those values from measured elapsed-time conclusions.

These findings support narrow corrections: name the artifact's lifecycle stage, use generated manifests, inspect available capability documentation, verify every material acceptance condition and audit the measurements themselves. They do not justify claiming that more review or higher effort always helps.

## Final pilot disposition

The independent reviewer recommended local acceptance after the corrected copy and missing per-cell assertions were supplied. The lead checked the exact final hashes, evidence linkage and scope. The final product correction changed only two dictionary values; previous navigation, invariant, build and journey evidence was carried with unchanged logic hashes. The affected copy, empty-create and three draft checks were exercised in all eight configurations.

Five normalized navigation segments required [1, 1, 2, 2, 2] actions before and [1, 1, 1, 1, 1] afterward. The three eligible routes each removed one tab/focus action. These are five bounded segments, not the total first-lesson journey or measured teacher time.

A deliberately invalid internal selection remained source/unit-test-bound; it was not fabricated through browser state mutation. Review used actual browser checks for reachable states and test/source evidence for that synthetic state.

The process trial is closed with the efficiency conclusion deferred: a working local increment was delivered and failures were caught, but this single pilot has no matched comparison and required considerable correction and coordination. Quality acceptance does not prove lower total effort.
